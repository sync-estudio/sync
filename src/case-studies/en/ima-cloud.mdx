## A technical operation living in loose files

**IMA Soluciones** is a Mexican company with more than 20 years in railway electrification, electromechanical maintenance and engineering for energy and telecommunications. Its teams coordinate technical projects inside and outside Mexico.

IMA's real operation was alive — but the information that holds it up lived in loose files, messages and paper forms. Bottlenecks hidden in plain sight: every report was captured twice, every piece of evidence was searched for in the wrong place, and every new project started by reinventing the operation.

---

## The challenge

Critical documentation living across local folders, messages and paper forms.

- Repeated capture of information across teams and projects.
- Historical reports hard to find when they were needed.
- Evidence scattered across local folders and internal messaging.
- Processes impossible to scale at the pace of the operation.

---

![Capturing operational reports from the platform](../../assets/case-studies/ima/report-work.webp)

## The solution

We didn't deliver software and walk away. We assessed the operation, built the tool and trained the team — all three together, because delivering only the code would have moved the problem, not solved it.

SYNC developed **IMA Cloud**, an internal web platform that centralizes reports, documentation and inventory in one place — designed around how IMA actually works, not the other way around.

- **Capture once, consult from any team.** Information stops being duplicated across forms and tools.
- **Evidence, reports and inventory in one system.** Goodbye to "which folder is this in?".
- **Dynamic templates that adapt to the real work.** Each report type carries its own structure without breaking the flow.

---

## Key features

### Dynamic reporting engine

Instead of rigid forms, IMA Cloud handles flexible structures for each type of operational report. Technical teams capture information in digital templates adapted to the activity, not to the tool.

- Templates with dynamic fields per report.
- Immediate access to the history.
- Organization by categories and operational modules.

![Operational reports queryable from the platform](../../assets/case-studies/ima/report-warehouse.webp)

### Drive-style document management

An internal repository where operational evidence and documents stop being scattered across local folders, messages and external tools.

- Upload and organization by folders.
- Centralization of evidence by project.
- Quick access from any team.

![Internal document system](../../assets/case-studies/ima/file-system.webp)

### Inventory control

Inventory connected to the operation, not as a separate spreadsheet. Materials, resources and movements are recorded alongside the rest of the project information.

- Recording and querying of stock.
- Traceability of movements.
- Integration with reports and documentation.

## How we worked with IMA

### 1. Assessment

Two weeks inside IMA's operation: where the work got stuck, which data lived in loose files, which processes were still alive out of habit. We came out with a plan, not a sales proposal.

### 2. Development

We built the dynamic reporting engine, document management and inventory in short sprints. Every release went through IMA's team before it touched their day-to-day — what didn't solve the real problem didn't stay in production.

### 3. Partnership

Workshops by role, playbooks versioned with the code, and maintenance of the system in production on our side. Monthly Support Plan included.

## Impact

IMA Cloud moved IMA Soluciones from an operation scattered across loose files to a centralized, traceable digital base. Nothing moved by inertia: each module stayed because the team decided it was useful.

- Operational reports in a single system, not scattered files.
- Evidence and documents queryable from any team.
- Digital capture in dynamic templates, no physical forms.
- Historical information available without depending on who saved it.
- Modular architecture ready to add new processes on the same base.
